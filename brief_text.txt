STALLION MARATHON
Concept, Programme & Packages  —  Content Brief for Brochure Design
Chapter I   ·   14 March 2027   ·   Lemerian Workin Café, Banjara Hills, Hyderabad
“Khoj Khud Ki. Untame Your Strength.”
1.  Overview
The Stallion Marathon is a multi-distance community run hosted by Lemerian Workin Café, India's first Workin Café. It is built on one idea: everyone carries a strength they have never fully let loose. The run is where they find it.
The marathon takes its identity from the stallion — a symbol of raw power, freedom and pedigree — carrying forward the Khoj Khud Ki (“discover yourself”) story that sits at the heart of everything Lemerian does.
2.  The Concept
Theme
Khoj Khud Ki — the search for one's own strength.
Metaphor
A stallion is not born tamed. Its power is natural, and what it chooses to do with it is its own. The run asks participants to stop holding back, whatever their distance or pace.
Why It Fits Lemerian
Lemerian Workin Café is a place where people have rebuilt their work and their confidence. The marathon extends that story from the café into the city, with the café as the start line, finish line and home base.
Tone
Emotional, storytelling-led and inspiring, with a premium sport feel. Lemerian is positioned as a beacon of light and hope.

4.  Brand Identity
Name: Stallion Marathon, with the tagline Khoj Khud Ki beneath it.
Colour: Deep royal blue as the signature colour across brochure, bibs and merchandise.
Mark: A minimal single-line horse silhouette beside a clean wordmark, in a luxury-sport style.
Tier language: a horse-gait ladder, where each step up means more speed and more access:
Trot — general entry
Canter — premium entry with workshops
VIP Circle — the top, capped tier (see Section 10)
Name alternatives considered: Thoroughbred, Derby and Equus were reviewed. Stallion was chosen for its raw, powerful feel and its direct link to the Khoj Khud Ki story.
5.  The Run — Vision Copy
Suggested headline: “Born To Run, Free.”
A stallion is never tamed — only trained. It channels its strength instead of losing it, and that discipline is what turns raw power into a clean, fast stride.
High-performing professionals carry the same instinct, and ignore it just as often. Deadlines come first, symptoms get waved off, and by the time the body insists on being heard, the cost has already shown up everywhere — in focus, in output, in the work itself.
Stallion is built on a simple belief: the sharpest minds belong in the strongest bodies. On 14th March 2027, Hyderabad's working professionals trade the desk for the start line — not to escape the grind, but to meet it stronger. On-ground health check-ups with practising doctors catch what a packed calendar lets slide, and a full field of distances means there's a pace for whoever shows up, whatever shape they arrive in.
“The strongest stride is still the one you choose for yourself.”

10.  The VIP Circle — “Black Stallion Circle” (₹50,000)
Positioning
The VIP tier is not a race entry. It is founding patronage of Chapter I: a one-time, limited, numbered seat at the start of something new. Chapter I can never be repeated, and early patrons carry that status permanently.
Working Name
Black Stallion Circle. The “Black” separates it from the event name so it keeps its exclusive feel. Alternatives under consideration: Founders' Stable and The Paddock.
Structure
Capped at 25 seats, numbered Stallion #01 to #25
Live seat count shown on the event page (for example, “11 of 25 remaining”)
Chapter I buyers carry the title Founding Stallion permanently
What the Buyer Gets
Private lounge at Lemerian on race day, with a dedicated host
Pre-run dinner or breakfast with the founder, the team, sponsors and workshop speakers
Workshops included
Personalised bib and finisher keepsake
Coffee hamper from the Lemerian sourcing model
Lemerian coworking pass or café credit after the event
Name on the Lemerian wall, in the brochure and on the event page
Photography and video they can reuse
Why ₹50,000 Is Justified
Founding status — Chapter I is a one-time window, and rates will rise for Chapter II.
Access — the room matters as much as the run: founders, sponsors, media and speakers in one place.
Scarcity — only 25 seats, stated publicly, with numbered ownership.
Visibility — for companies, recognition, content and a media mention make this cheaper than a typical sponsorship.
Purpose — every seat funds something specific and verifiable, such as sponsored participants or workshops.
Personalisation — a named host, a custom bib and a handwritten note make the buyer feel looked after.
Lasting value — the coworking pass and coffee hamper outlast the day.
Team value — a corporate bundle of 2 to 4 seats works as a leadership wellness experience.
Pitch Line
“Stallions aren't born tamed. They're untamed by choice. Twenty-five people will stand at the front of Chapter I. Stallion #__ is yours.”
Likely Objection
“Why not just donate ₹50,000?” — Because the VIP seat is the donation plus the experience, the network and the recognition.
Pricing Structure
The full ladder runs: race entries, then premium entries with workshops, then the VIP Circle at ₹50,000. The middle tiers anchor the top one, so it reads as the top of a scale rather than an outlier.
11.  Audience & Lead Generation
General runners — Hyderabad's running and fitness community, using the 1 to 21 KM range so every level has an entry point.
Students — college events, including a stall at the GRIET GDGOC event. Two 6-foot flyers will anchor the stall; table size, water and charging outlets (with wattage) are to be confirmed with organisers.
Corporates — team entries and VIP bundles, positioned as team wellness and employer branding.
Founders and HNIs — the VIP Circle, sold through personal outreach rather than public ads.
12.  Sponsors, Media & the Proof Story
Sponsors and media find it hard to believe that India's first Workin Café exists and has changed lives. The answer is evidence.
Testimonial case study — written testimonials from Lemerian regulars, each with signature, image and consent.
Testimony prompts — what you do and where you work, how many times you have visited, and what impact Lemerian has had on your life.
Impact message — “Your effort will impact more than 20,000 people.” Only publish impact figures that can be backed up.
Use — the case study leads the sponsor deck, the VIP pitch and the media outreach.

A formal invitation is being prepared for the Chief Minister of Telangana to grace the event as Chief Guest. A government guest raises credibility with sponsors and media, and supports the VIP pitch.
